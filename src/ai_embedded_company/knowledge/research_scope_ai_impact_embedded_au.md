## 1. Research Questions & Scope

### Primary Research Question
**How is AI (artificial intelligence / machine learning) reshaping embedded and firmware engineering roles in the Australian job market?**

### Sub-Questions
1. **Employment volume** — Is AI creating or destroying embedded/firmware roles in Australia? What is the net effect?
2. **Skill shifts** — What new skills are becoming essential? Which traditional skills are being de-emphasized?
3. **Salary impact** — Are AI-adjacent skills commanding salary premiums? How large?
4. **Role evolution** — Are entirely new roles emerging (e.g., Edge AI Engineer)? Are existing roles disappearing?
5. **Entry-level impact** — How is AI changing hiring patterns for junior vs. senior engineers?
6. **Geographic/industry variation** — Which Australian sectors (defence, medtech, energy, automotive) are most affected?

## 2. Key Findings (Executed Research)

### 2.1 AI Is Adding Jobs, Not Replacing Them

The Australian embedded/firmware market is structurally undersupplied:

- **18%** of surveyed Australian organisations increased hiring budgets due to AI; only **3%** reduced headcount (Clicks IT Recruitment, Jan 2025)
- The **Reserve Bank of Australia** (Nov 2025, 100+ firms) found AI adoption results in a "net increase in employment" for Australia
- Big Wave Digital (Australian embedded recruiter, 2026): *"Physical products cannot be built by prompt alone, supply of experienced engineers remains tight"*

### 2.2 New Role: Edge AI Engineer Commands Premium

The hottest new role is the **Edge AI Engineer** — deploying ML models on MCUs:

| Role | Mid-Level | Senior | Principal/Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge AI Engineer** | $130k–$160k | $160k–$200k | $200k–$240k |
| Firmware Engineer | $115k–$145k | $145k–$185k | $185k–$225k |
| Embedded Linux Engineer | $120k–$150k | $150k–$190k | $190k–$230k |

Edge AI Engineers earn **~$10k–$15k more** than traditional firmware peers (Big Wave Digital 2026 salary guide).

### 2.3 Skills in Demand

| Skill | Premium Reason |
|---|---|
| TinyML / Edge AI | Porting models to MCUs — highest salary premium |
| Rust for embedded | Safety-critical firmware — fast-growing |
| Embedded Linux + Yocto | Smart-device platform layer |
| Zephyr RTOS | Modern RTOS — rapidly adopted |
| Secure boot / TPM / OTA | Device security is non-negotiable |
| FPGA (VHDL/Verilog) | Defence, space, signal processing |
| AI-assisted workflows | Expected in senior roles |

### 2.4 Three Structural Market Drivers

1. **Defence & Space** — SKA telescope, quantum sensing, satellite payloads, underwater navigation
2. **Energy Transition** — Firmware in batteries, EV chargers, inverters, grid infrastructure
3. **Intelligent Edge** — ML models running on microcontrollers everywhere

### 2.5 Entry-Level Disruption

- **ReadyTech** told the government it now hires *"fewer entry-level engineers"* — senior engineers with AI tools outperform multiple juniors
- Engineers Australia notes **41% of Australians are uncomfortable with AI** in hiring, creating demand for ethics/governance roles

### 2.6 AI Tools Are Becoming Standard Infrastructure

- **89.3% of companies** use AI-powered coding assistants (Black Duck 2025)
- **96.1%** embed open-source AI models into products
- Engineers reported **25%+ efficiency** improvements from AI-assisted coding (ReadyTech data)
- Firms are actively hiring for: AI Product Manager (35%), AI Governance Specialist (29%), Data Scientist (28%), AI Engineer (28%)

## 4. Conclusion

AI is not a threat to embedded/firmware engineering employment in Australia. The market is **structurally undersupplied**, and AI is creating a **new, well-compensated skill layer** on top of traditional embedded fundamentals. Engineers who combine strong C/C++/RTOS skills with Edge AI deployment capabilities are seeing the strongest demand and salary growth.

The key risk is not to the profession as a whole, but to engineers who **fail to adopt AI tools** — these are becoming standard infrastructure expected at senior levels.
