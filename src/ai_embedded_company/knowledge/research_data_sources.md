# Data Sources for AI Impact on Australian Embedded/Firmware Engineering Research

## Australian-Specific Sources

| Source | Type | Data Available | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Big Wave Digital** (bigwavedigital.com.au) | Embedded recruitment specialist | 2026 salary guide, market trends | Public |
| **SEEK** (seek.com.au) | Job board | Job posting volumes, salary ranges | Public API |
| **LinkedIn Jobs** | Job board | Role counts, skill demand trends | Public |
| **Robert Half** (roberthalf.com) | Recruitment agency | Annual salary guides, hiring surveys | Public reports |
| **Clicks IT Recruitment** | Recruitment agency | Annual IT recruitment report | Public |
| **Reserve Bank of Australia** | Central bank | AI & Labour Market survey (Nov 2025) | Public |
| **Jobs and Skills Australia** | Government agency | Generative AI Capacity Study | Public |
| **Engineers Australia** | Professional body | Pulse Report, member surveys | Public |
| **Australian Bureau of Statistics** | Government | Employment data by occupation | Public |
