> **Date**: 2026-07-08

## The Bottom Line

**AI is not a threat to embedded/firmware engineering employment in Australia.** The market is structurally undersupplied, and AI is creating a new, well-compensated skill layer on top of traditional embedded fundamentals.

## Three Structural Market Drivers

1. **Defence & Space** — SKA telescope, quantum sensing, satellite payloads, autonomous navigation
2. **Energy Transition** — EV chargers, battery management, grid infrastructure firmware
3. **Intelligent Edge** — ML on MCUs for vision, audio, vibration analysis

These drivers are creating **more embedded roles** — not eliminating them.

---

## The Real Risk: Not AI, but Not Adopting AI

| Factor | Impact | Detail |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Total employment | **Positive** | Market undersupplied, AI adding net roles |
| Salary | **Positive** | Edge AI skills command $10k–$15k premium |
| Role complexity | **Positive** | More interesting work, higher bar |
| Entry-level access | **Negative** | Fewer junior positions; "missing rung" |
| Skill requirements | **Shift** | C/C++/RTOS still essential + Edge AI deployment |
| Job security | **Positive** | Hardware is real — can't be prompted away |

---

## What AI Still Cannot Do in Embedded

1. **System architecture tradeoffs** — HW/SW partitioning, power/performance/cost
2. **Hardware-level debugging** — Reading schematics, oscilloscope analysis
3. **Board bring-up** — Power sequence debugging, no-output startup
4. **Functional safety & compliance** — ISO 26262, IEC 61508, IEC 62304
5. **Novel/rare components** — Limited public data for training
6. **Large legacy codebases** — Product-specific assumptions AI can't trace

---

## Recommendations for Engineers

1. **Deepen C/C++ and RTOS fundamentals** — These remain the non-negotiable foundation
2. **Add Edge AI skills** — TensorFlow Lite Micro, ONNX Runtime, model quantisation
3. **Learn Rust for embedded** — Fast-growing for safety-critical firmware
4. **Embrace AI tools** — They're becoming standard infrastructure, not optional extras
5. **Target defence/energy/medtech** — Sectors with strongest embedded demand in Australia
