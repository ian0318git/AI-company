# Research Scope: AI Impact on Australian Embedded/Firmware Engineer Job Market

## 1. Research Questions

### Primary Question
**How will advances in AI (LLMs, code generation, autonomous debugging) affect the employment landscape for embedded/firmware engineers in Australia over the next 1–5 years?**

### Secondary Questions

1. **Task Displacement**: Which specific embedded/firmware engineering tasks are most susceptible to AI automation (driver development, HAL configuration, RTOS tuning, hardware validation, documentation)?
2. **Demand Shift**: Is AI increasing or decreasing overall demand for embedded engineers in Australia? Are new roles emerging (e.g., "AI-augmented firmware engineer")?
3. **Skill Premium**: What skills command a wage premium in the AI era — Rust expertise, ML on edge, formal verification, hardware/software co-design?
4. **Geographic Variation**: How does AI impact differ between Sydney/Melbourne vs. regional hubs (Brisbane, Adelaide, Newcastle) vs. remote work?
5. **Industry Sector Variation**: AI impact across Australian embedded sectors — medtech (Cochlear, ResMed), agtech, mining automation, consumer IoT, defence?
6. **Education Pipeline**: How are Australian universities (UNSW, RMIT, UQ, Adelaide) adapting embedded curriculum for the AI era? Is the gap widening between graduate capability and industry need?
7. **Offshoring Risk**: Does AI accelerate or decelerate offshoring of embedded work from Australia to lower-cost markets?
8. **Immigration & Visa Dynamics**: Interaction with Australia's skilled migration program (482/494 visas) for embedded engineers — does AI reduce the skills gap argument?

## 2. Scope Boundaries

### In Scope
- Australian labour market for embedded/firmware engineers (ANZSCO code 233411 / 263311 related)
- Time horizon: 2026–2031
- C/C++, Rust, Assembly, and hardware-near Python roles
- Microcontroller (MCU), DSP, FPGA, and SoC-level firmware
- RTOS (FreeRTOS, Zephyr, VxWorks) and bare-metal development
- AI-assisted development tools: GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex
- Embedded CI/CD, HIL testing, and validation tooling
- Australian industry sectors: medical devices, mining/automation, agriculture, defence, consumer electronics, telecommunications

### Out of Scope
- IT/cloud/data-centre software engineering roles (non-embedded)
- Frontend, mobile app, and pure web development
- Hardware design (PCB, analogue, RF) unless directly coupled with firmware
- Non-Australian labour markets except as comparative reference
- General AI ethics and existential risk debates
- Hardware manufacturing job impact (pick-and-place, assembly line)
