# MVP Scope: 企業數位轉型與自動化 — AI 代理人策略辯論

**Pipeline**: quick-prototype | **Type**: Strategy Debate | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

## Deliverable Overview

This project produces a strategic analysis debating two approaches to enterprise AI adoption:
- **全面 AI 代理人替代方針** (Full AI Agent Replacement): Automate workflows, reduce 30% of junior headcount
- **人機協同方針** (Human-AI Collaboration): No layoffs, AI as assistant, upskill entire workforce

---

## MVP Deliverable Structure

### 1. Debate Framework
- **Opening Statements**: Each side stakes their position (~300 words each)
- **Dimension A — Cost & Efficiency**
  - Automation advocate: numbers on task volume, error rates, 24/7 coverage, cost savings
  - Collaboration advocate: hidden costs of AI failures, escalation needs, institutional knowledge loss
- **Dimension B — Quality & Innovation**
  - Automation advocate: consistency, speed, freeing humans for creative work
  - Collaboration advocate: human judgment in edge cases, AI as augmentation not replacement
- **Dimension C — Workforce & Culture**
  - Automation advocate: competitive necessity, reallocate budget to strategic hires
  - Collaboration advocate: morale, employer brand, retraining ROI, social responsibility
- **Dimension D — Risk & Compliance**
  - Automation advocate: reduced human error in compliance tasks
  - Collaboration advocate: AI hallucination risk, accountability gaps, regulatory uncertainty

### 2. CEO Ruling
- Phased hybrid approach recommended
- Phase 1 (0-6 months): AI assistant deployment +全员AI培训 (universal AI literacy training)
- Phase 2 (6-12 months): Automate proven-safe workflows, measure savings
- Phase 3 (12-18 months): Strategic headcount decisions based on data, not arbitrary 30% target

### 3. Key Metrics to Track
- Task completion time (before/after AI)
- Error rates and escalation volume
- Employee AI proficiency scores
- Customer satisfaction with AI-assisted support
- Cost per task processed

---

## Success Criteria
1. Both positions argued with concrete data and organizational theory
2. CEO ruling provides a phased, measurable path (not binary choice)
3. Document usable as executive briefing material
4. Risk analysis covers both technical and human dimensions
