# MVP Scope: 年度績效與管理評級制度辯論

**Pipeline**: quick-prototype | **Type**: Management Strategy Debate | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

## Deliverable Overview

This project produces a structured management strategy debate document analyzing the optimal performance review and rating system for an organization. The debate pits **Results_Director** (manager-driven ratings) against **Culture_Specialist** (360-degree peer review).

---

## MVP Deliverable Structure

### 1. Debate Framework (Core)
- **Opening Statements**: Each role establishes their fundamental position (~300 words each)
- **Round 1**: Dimension A — "Rating Objectivity & True Contribution Alignment"
  - Results_Director argues manager has full context of direct reports' output
  - Culture_Specialist argues peers see collaboration that managers miss
- **Round 2**: Dimension B — "Preventing Office Politics & Favoritism"
  - Results_Director argues peer reviews create popularity contests
  - Culture_Specialist argues manager-only reviews enable bias and abuse
- **Round 3**: Dimension C — "Long-term Trust Impact on Teams"
  - Results_Director argues clear accountability builds trust
  - Culture_Specialist argues 360 transparency builds psychological safety
- **Closing Ruling**: CHRO (Chief Human Resources Officer) synthesizes into an integrated system design

### 2. Integrated System Design (CHRO Ruling)
- Hybrid model: Manager rating (60% weight) + Peer review (40% weight)
- Calibration committee for cross-team fairness
- Anonymous peer feedback with structured rubrics
- Quarterly check-ins supplement annual review
- Appeal mechanism for contested ratings

### 3. Implementation Roadmap
- Phase 1: Pilot with 2 departments (3 months)
- Phase 2: Company-wide rollout with training (6 months)
- Phase 3: First full review cycle + retrospective (12 months)

---

## Success Criteria
1. Both roles engage substantively across all 3 dimensions
2. CHRO ruling is specific and actionable, not a vague compromise
3. Document can serve as a board-ready proposal
4. Organizational behavior and management psychology principles are cited
